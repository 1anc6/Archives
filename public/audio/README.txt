The site plays its own built-in cosy tracks (generated in the browser), so nothing is needed here.

To add your own songs, put the MP3 files in this folder and list them in data/site.json, e.g.
  "music": [
    { "title": "Late night jazz", "note": "Brushes and upright bass", "file": "audio/late-night-jazz.mp3" }
  ]
They appear at the top of the music menu, before the built-in tracks.
Only use music you're allowed to share (your own, or royalty-free / Creative Commons with credit).
