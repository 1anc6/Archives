    Lanchenba Mutum

    Lanchenba Mutum is the author of this collection of twelve mathematical essays. The work explores unconventional questions in mathematics, from the different sizes of infinity and the equality 0.999… = 1 to the limits of proof, probability, and patterns in nature. By connecting foundational ideas with anime, music, LEGO and Secret Santa, Lanchenba offers clear, original perspectives that make complex mathematics engaging and accessible.

      Using this site

      • Reading online. Select any essay title to open its page, then choose Read essay. Your place is saved automatically, so you may return to it at any time.
      • Reading list. Select the bookmark on an essay to add it to your reading list in the Library.
      • Downloading. Every essay is available as a PDF from the reader or the Library.
      • Reading comfort. The reader offers light, sepia and dark modes. Optional background music and sound effects can be switched on or off in the header.
