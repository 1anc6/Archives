            Lanchenba Mutum

            Author · The Open Archive

            Lanchenba Mutum is the author of this collection of twelve mathematical essays. The work explores unconventional questions in mathematics, from the different sizes of infinity and the equality 0.999… = 1 to the limits of proof, probability, and patterns in nature. By connecting foundational ideas with anime, music, LEGO and Secret Santa, Lanchenba offers clear, original perspectives that make complex mathematics engaging and accessible.

            From the Author

            Hi, I'm Lanchenba Mutum, or just Lance if you'd rather not wrestle with all those syllables (most people don't, and honestly, fair lol). This is where my maths essays live. Full disclosure: the Jujutsu Kaisen one exists purely because I was enjoying the anime way too much and thought, "wait, Gojo's Infinity is basically a convergent series" lol. The rest happened the same way. A box of LEGO, a Secret Santa draw, the number 73, and suddenly I'm writing pages about limits, the sizes of infinity, Gödel and game theory.

            They're written for anyone who's curious, no maths degree required (there are fewer scary symbols than it looks, I promise lol). Want a PDF? Tap Request PDF and send me a quick email, I'm happy to share. Questions, corrections and ideas for the next essay are very welcome too: lanchenba613500@gmail.com.

            Using this site

            • Reading online. Select any essay title to open its page, then choose Read essay. Your place is saved automatically, so you may return to it at any time.
            • Highlights. In the reader, select text and choose Highlight. Your highlights stay on the page and are collected in the Library.
            • Reading list. Select the bookmark on an essay to add it to your reading list in the Library.
            • PDFs. Every essay is free to read here. For the PDF, select Request PDF on an essay page, in the reader or in the Library, and send the short email that opens. If you don't use an email app, choose Copy the request instead.
            • New essays. Select Email me new essays at the bottom of the Essays page and send the email that opens. You'll hear about the next essay as soon as it's out.
            • Reading comfort. The reader offers light, sepia and dark modes. Optional background music and sound effects can be switched on or off in the header.
